Product Roadmap

Built to grow
with you.

PayCircle ships as a complete MVP and evolves into a full financial intelligence platform in three planned phases.

Phase 1 — Now
Core MVP
  • User registration & JWT auth
  • Group creation & member management
  • Equal & exact expense splitting
  • Real-time balance engine
  • Manual settlement recording
  • Personal income & expense tracking
  • Unified dashboard & budget tracking
Phase 2 — Advanced
Intelligence Layer
  • Settlement optimization (min cash flow)
  • Percentage & share-based splits
  • Recurring expenses with auto-add
  • Edit & delete expenses
  • Push notifications & smart alerts
  • Spending charts & visual analytics
  • Rule-based behavioral insights
Phase 3 — Future
Premium Features
  • UPI integration & QR code settlements
  • Receipt OCR for auto-entry
  • Data export — PDF & CSV reports
  • AI-powered spending predictions
  • Multi-currency support
  • Category-wise budgets with rollover
  • Social & collaborative features
