Delete your PayCircle account

PayCircle · com.paycircle.app

You can delete your PayCircle account and its data at any time, either from inside the app or by sending us a request. This page explains both routes and exactly what is removed.

Deleting from inside the app

  1. Open PayCircle and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.
  4. Tap Delete My Account.

You are signed out immediately and cannot sign in again. The deletion is irreversible — we cannot restore an account once it has been deleted.

Requesting deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email iakshatdubey@gmail.com from the address registered on your account, with the subject Account deletion request. We will confirm your identity and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

PayCircle records shared expenses. Some records involve other people, and removing them outright would change what those people owe each other. We therefore delete everything that is yours alone and strip your identity from anything that is shared.

DataWhat happens
Name, email address, password, username, phone, avatar, UPI ID Deleted. Your email and username are released and can be registered again.
Personal transactions and budgets Deleted permanently.
Your private expense categories Deleted permanently.
Friend connections and pending requests Deleted permanently.
Personal contacts you created, and splits with them Deleted permanently. These were visible only to you.
Notification token Deleted. No further notifications are sent.
Groups where you were the only member Deleted along with their expenses and settlements.
Group expenses, settlements and splits involving other people Kept, shown as “Deleted user”. Other members' balances depend on these amounts, so the figures remain while your identity is removed.
Groups you administered Administrator role passes to another member so the group stays usable.
Settle up first. Deleting your account does not cancel money you owe or are owed. Those amounts stay in your groups' balances under “Deleted user”. Settle outstanding balances before you delete if you want them cleared.

Retention

Data marked as deleted above is removed from our database at the time of deletion, not queued for later erasure. Routine encrypted database backups may retain copies for up to 30 days, after which they expire. Retained shared records carry no personal identifiers.
