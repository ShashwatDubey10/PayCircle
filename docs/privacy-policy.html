PayCircle Privacy Policy

PayCircle · com.paycircle.app · Last updated: 29 October 2026

PayCircle is an expense-splitting app for sharing costs within a group. This policy explains what it collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. It covers the PayCircle Android app and the service behind it.

PayCircle does not move money. It records who paid what and works out who owes whom. It never connects to a bank account, never processes a payment, and never asks for card, bank or UPI PIN credentials. A UPI ID, if you choose to add one, is stored only so the app can generate a QR code that your own UPI app can scan. The payment happens entirely in that other app.

2. What we do not collect

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind. The app contains no advertising, analytics or attribution SDKs, and we do not build advertising profiles.
  • No access to your device's contact list. “Personal contacts” in PayCircle are names you type in yourself.
  • No photos. The camera is used only to scan a UPI QR code. The image is decoded on your device, never captured, stored or transmitted.
  • No location data.
  • No bank or card credentials.

3. How your information is shared

With other PayCircle users

This is the point of the app, so it is worth being explicit. Members of a group you join can see your name, the expenses you add, what you paid, your share of each expense and your balance with them. Friends you connect with can see one-to-one splits between you. If you add a UPI ID, people who owe you can see it in the payment QR code.

Your personal transactions, budgets and private expense categories are never visible to anyone else.

4. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never stored in readable form.
  • Sessions use signed tokens that expire after seven days.
  • Access to the database is restricted to the application.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong password that you do not reuse elsewhere.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app, or by emailing us.

Because PayCircle records shared expenses, deletion treats private and shared data differently: everything that is yours alone is deleted permanently, while records that other people's balances depend on are kept with your identity removed and shown as “Deleted user”. The full breakdown is on our account deletion page.

Encrypted database backups may retain copies for up to 30 days before expiring. Server logs are retained by our hosting provider on their own schedule.

6. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and edit your profile in the app
  • Delete individual transactions, contacts and splits
  • Delete your entire account
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you, by email

7. Children

PayCircle is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be announced in the app.
